## `induced-clutter` — DONE 2026-09-15 (`bc9cb50`)

Induced edges are out of the relation bar, the legend and the Ownership tour;
they serve layout only.

⚠️ **Two different mechanisms are both called "induced" — keep them straight.**

(i) `inducedFrom` (ours, `containmentGraph.ts`) retargets a forward-owned edge
at each subclass of its RANGE. Nothing declares the slot, so it is an inference,
not a declaration; filtered in `collectRelations`.

(ii) `inherited_from` (LinkML's `SchemaView.induced_class()`, `induced_schema.py`)
copies a parent's slot onto each subclass of the DECLARING class, so all of them
genuinely hold it; a merged box unions its members and repeats the row once per
member, which `buildRelationRows` collapses. **That collapse is keyed on the
ROWS, not on `inheritedFrom`** — a subclass may re-declare a slot via
`slot_usage` with a narrower range (`DimensionalObservationSet.observations →
DimensionalObservation`), and that row is a different fact that must survive.

Three tests in `src/test/relationBar.test.ts` pin both, plus the re-declare case
that distinguishes them.

**Note on the original row:** it named `inducedFrom` as the cause of
ObservationSet's "4 distinct entities through 13 attributes" and prescribed one
filter in `RelationBar.tsx`. Both were wrong — not one of those 13 rows was an
induced edge, and `RelationEntry` carries no `inducedFrom` to filter on. The row
had been written from a screenshot rather than from the data.
