## `popover-placement` — DONE 2026-09-19

`make e2e-probe` is **5/5**, from the 2 pass / 3 fail baseline the row named as
its constraint; the two previously-passing tests stayed passing, and the result
held over four consecutive runs.

It was **two unrelated faults** wearing one symptom, which is why three earlier
fixes aimed at placement all missed:

1. **A top-layer popover too tall for its `position-area` cell is moved back
   inside the viewport by the browser.** `fitToRoom` bounds its height to the
   room on the side it was placed on, and the body scrolls — so the authored
   side holds and the nav row stays reachable.
2. **The back-step delta was not a placement bug.** The popover was correctly
   12px below its anchor both ways; the CANVAS had scrolled, because a beat
   re-shows its anchor only when the anchor value changes and consecutive beats
   often share one.

⚠️ **The mechanism recorded by the previous two sessions was wrong**: it is the
TOP LAYER that triggers the clamp, not `position: fixed`. A plain fixed box
never slid at any height. See WORKLOG 2026-09-19 (later still) for the
measurement and for why "the top layer itself" was closed too early.

What must stay true of any future change is in
[BACKLOG §Placement](../BACKLOG.md#placement). ⚠️ Whether the tours READ well
through a back-step is still unverified — that is `read-tours`.
